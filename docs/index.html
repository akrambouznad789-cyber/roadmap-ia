AI Roadmap · two classes in parallel

AI Roadmap

Two 140-day intensive classes, run at the same time. One learns to build with models, from data up to a language model in service; the other learns to run them on your own machine, from memory up to a private appliance. Theory first, one page a day, a project every 7 to 10 days, and every project aims at a model that runs locally.

The two classes

Before the classes

Three applied projects and two stations, done before the two classes; their write-ups are in French. Each project is named after its methods; the dataset is only the practical case.

  • P1 · published

    Boosting, validation and leakage

    gradient boosting · time-based validation · leakage detection · permutation importance

    Case: late deliveries (DataCo, 180,519 rows). Leakage detected and removed; 72.2% accuracy and an AUC of 0.774 on the test set.

  • P2 · published

    Clustering and difference-in-differences

    k-means · PCA · difference-in-differences · parallel trends · placebo test

    Case: cycling in Montreal. Three profiles of BIXI stations; effect of the Saint-Denis REV bike lane on the Brébeuf path: −38% to −64% in cyclist counts.

  • P3 · published

    PyTorch network and embeddings

    PyTorch · embeddings · gradient boosting · time-based validation · local measurement

    Case: BIXI trip durations. Boosting stays the most accurate (3.28 min of error on the test set); the network follows at 3.34 min, 4 times lighter. Without the distance, its embeddings partly redraw the map of Montreal.

  • P4 · in progress

    BPE tokenization and a mini-Transformer

    Started; it ends during phase 3 of the Applied AI Intensive Class, once attention has been covered in theory.

  • Station 1 · published

    Neural networks: the multilayer perceptron

    dense layers · ReLU, sigmoid · MSE cost · stochastic gradient descent · mini-batches · normalization

    Case: estimating the 30th-highest hourly volume from Québec's public traffic counts.

  • Station 2 · published

    Backpropagation and automatic differentiation

    chain rule · computation graph · gradients · autograd

    Case: a home-made backpropagation engine, checked against PyTorch on Québec's public traffic counts.
